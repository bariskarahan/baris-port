# Mobile gaming visual research

Reviewed 9 October 2026. This research informs illustration subjects and their placement. It does not turn the site's sample analyses into verified research or establish causal effects on retention.

## Industry context

Sensor Tower's public 2026 report overview emphasizes retention, engagement and monetization as downloads decline, and identifies strategy as a strong genre. Its public 2025 Live Ops article cautions that events alone do not guarantee growth. The full gated reports were not accessed. Unity's May 2025 article discusses combining in-app purchases and advertising. These sources support studying product systems rather than using generic controller imagery. They do not establish that every mobile game has the same economy or monetization model.

## Puzzle onboarding and moment-to-moment play

Royal Match's developer listing describes match-3 levels, obstacles, boosters and event rewards. Dream Games documents coins earned from levels, chests and events, and spent on lives, moves and boosters. King's help center describes boosters used before, during or after a level, earned or purchased. The relevant visual vocabulary is a portrait touch-screen puzzle board, goals, finite moves and a small booster inventory.

- https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.dreamgames.royalmatch
- https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/21-how-can-i-obtain-and-use-coins/
- https://candycrush.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000750998-What-are-Boosters

Placement: hero introduction, featured onboarding note, puzzle teardown. Replace retro handheld-console imagery with an original mobile puzzle illustration.

## Progression, economies and return goals

Supercell's December 2023 Brawl Pass design update discusses season length, more flexible seasonal quests, and reward tracks for free and paying players. It is a historical design case, not current pricing or a current balance specification. Dream Games' currency documentation supports depicting coins, lives and boosters as distinct resources. A level-node path and reward track provide a clearer metaphor than an ornamental crystal staircase or a balance scale.

Placement: progression, game economy, setback and comeback notes; the status-versus-currency experiment. Represent generic milestones and currencies without invented prices or asserted game results.

## Social mechanics and live operations

Scopely's June 2024 Tycoon Racers announcement documents teams of four, collecting flags, lap milestones, player-chosen rewards and competition over a limited event. This supports imagery of four player slots, a shared objective, progress toward a chest, and event competition. It does not establish that small teams cause higher retention; that remains a proposed experiment.

- https://www.scopely.com/en/news/team-up-with-friends-and-conquer-the-monopoly-go-race-track-with-tycoon-racers

Placement: small-group experiment on the homepage and experiments page. Replace the progression illustration previously attached to the social question.
